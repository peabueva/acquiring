Glossary

Acquiring
A bank service that lets a business accept card payments and receive the money on its account.
Acquirer
The merchant's bank. It processes payments and settles funds to the merchant.
Issuer
The bank that issued the customer's card. It approves or declines each payment.
Card network
Visa, Mastercard and others: route messages between acquirer and issuer.
3-D Secure
An extra check where the customer confirms the payment with the issuer (code, app push).
Authorization
The issuer reserves the amount on the card. Money moves later.
Settlement
Usually 1–3 days later, the acquirer transfers the funds, minus its fee, to the merchant.
